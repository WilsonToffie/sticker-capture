Sticker Capture privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026. Developer: Van Den Berg Studios.

What the app does

The app lets you select visible screen content, save still or animated stickers, organise local sticker packs and add them to WhatsApp. It does not require an account or a TikTok login.

Screen access and permissions

Android screen-sharing consent lets the app capture the visible screen during an approved session. While recording an animation, a temporary recording of the display is stored in private app storage so the selected area can be extracted. Do not display content you do not want included while capturing. The microphone is not recorded.

The appear-on-top permission displays the floating capture controls. The foreground service keeps the active session visible; notification permission allows the ongoing capture notification. Use the floating × or End session in the notification to stop screen sharing. Android may require fresh consent when a session restarts.

Optional sticker assistant

The sticker assistant is off by default. Enabling it requires a separate in-app explanation and your approval in Android Accessibility settings. It inspects visible interface elements in supported TikTok apps to suggest sticker areas. When accessible image bounds are unavailable in a recognised comments panel, it may take an occasional screen image and analyse it locally. Detection can run while the app’s main screen is closed.

Visible element labels and detection images are used temporarily in memory. They are not saved, uploaded, included in backups or written to diagnostic logs. The assistant does not read passwords or editable fields, perform gestures, send messages or automatically save stickers. Technical diagnostics can include assistant permission state, scan counts and error codes, without screen text or images.

Tap a suggestion to check its crop and choose whether to capture it. Actual capture uses the same Android screen-sharing consent and local recording process described above; a recording session started by the assistant ends after the capture, cancellation or error. Disable Sticker assistant in the app’s Settings or revoke its access in Android Accessibility settings to stop detection. Recognition varies with TikTok’s interface, and regular floating capture remains available without enabling the assistant.

Local processing and storage

Capture and sticker preparation run on your phone. The app has no developer-operated capture-upload service, advertising SDK or analytics SDK. Saved original captures, crop-padding metadata, editable text, retained sources for edited stickers, sticker-pack images, pack names, preferences and preparation status are stored in private app storage.

The app also keeps a bounded local diagnostic log of technical events, permission checks and errors. It includes app and Android versions, device model, capture stages, codec details and Java error traces. It does not record screenshots, sticker images, caption text, chat content or hardware identifiers. Old log entries are automatically replaced; clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the private logs.

When you choose to share or export

Adding or updating a pack makes its prepared stickers and pack information available to WhatsApp. WhatsApp handles their subsequent use under its own policies. The app does not send an entire screen recording to WhatsApp.

Exporting a backup writes your saved original captures, edited-sticker sources and text metadata, sticker-pack copies and collection preferences to the location you choose in Android’s file picker. A chosen cloud-storage provider may upload that file under its own policies. Backup ZIP files are not encrypted by this app. Restoring reads a backup you select and adds its collection to this phone.

Contacting support opens your email app. The app does not automatically attach captures, backups or diagnostic logs. Your email provider and the support recipient handle anything you choose to send.

Export diagnostic log in Settings saves the technical log as a text file to the location you choose. Nothing is sent automatically. You may review the file and share it for troubleshooting; exported files remain until you delete them, and your chosen storage or messaging provider handles them under its own policies.

Retention and deletion

Saved originals and packs remain until you delete them or clear the app’s storage. Delete originals from a sticker preview or the selection controls in Stickers. Copies already in packs are kept separately; use the pack’s management controls to remove those copies or delete a pack. WhatsApp’s cached pack may need to be removed in WhatsApp too.

Temporary recordings are removed after normal capture completion, failure or session shutdown. A crash or forced termination can leave temporary files. The app cleans recognised abandoned recordings on a later start when no capture session is active; Storage & backup also provides Clear unused files. Current originals and pack assets are preserved by that cleanup.

Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes the app’s private collection. A backup you exported remains in its chosen location until you delete it there. There are no app accounts to delete.

This website and support email

The public privacy and support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its handling of service data. Visiting this website does not upload captures or diagnostic logs from the Android app.

Support email is handled through Gmail. Your sender address, message and any attachments you choose to send are processed by the email providers involved and used by us to respond to your request. See Google’s privacy policy for Gmail’s handling of data.

Contact and changes

Privacy and support contact: vandenbergstudiossupport@gmail.com. Support messages you send may be retained to resolve your request; request their deletion through this contact. Do not send private screen content unless you intend to share it with support.

Material changes to these practices will be reflected in the policy and its update date. The published policy and store disclosures must match the version distributed.
