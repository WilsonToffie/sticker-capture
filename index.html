Your stickers.
All in one place.

Capture still and moving stickers from visible screen content, add your own text, organise your sticker box, and add your packs to WhatsApp.

Sticker Capture for Android 13 and later. Currently in development and testing.

Keep a sticker in a few steps

  1. Start a capture session and approve Android’s screen-sharing request.
  2. Tap the floating capture button, choose +, then select the sticker and capture.
  3. Select saved stickers, choose or create a pack, then add or update that pack in WhatsApp.

Your collection, under your control

Capture and preparation run on your phone. You choose when to share a pack or export a collection backup.
