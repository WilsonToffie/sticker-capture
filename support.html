Help us investigate an error

  1. Try the action that failed.
  2. Open Settings → Export diagnostic log and save the text file.
  3. Attach that file to your support email, with a short description of what happened.

The log contains technical events and device details. It does not contain sticker images or screen recordings. You can review it before sharing.

Common questions

The floating button will not start

In your phone’s Settings, find Display over other apps or Appear on top, select Sticker Capture and enable it. On some phones Android opens a list first; select Sticker Capture in that list, then allow the permission. Return to the app and start a new capture session.

How do I enable sticker suggestions?

Open Settings → Sticker assistant in the app, choose Enable, read the separate disclosure and agree if you want to proceed. In Android Accessibility settings, select Sticker assistant and approve access. The assistant is off by default, and can be turned off in the app or Android settings. It suggests visible sticker areas in supported TikTok layouts. Google Images in Chrome can be enabled separately after its explanation: the browser address is checked locally to recognise supported Google Images pages, then image results and previews can receive suggestions. Other browser pages are excluded. Detection addresses and labels are not saved or uploaded; nothing is saved automatically. Tap a suggestion, approve screen sharing, check the crop and capture. A session started by the assistant ends after that capture. Some layouts may not be recognised; regular floating capture remains available.

For a manually installed APK, Android may restrict accessibility access. If Android shows that restriction, its documented route is Settings → Apps → Sticker Capture → More → Allow restricted settings. Only allow this if you trust the installed app.

Why does Android ask to share the screen again?

Each new capture session needs Android’s approval. Keep the current session running to capture more stickers. Locking the phone, stopping the session or updating the app may require fresh approval.

Where is my saved sticker?

Open Stickers to view your saved collection. Preparation continues in the background. Select stickers, then choose an existing pack or create a new one when you want to use them in WhatsApp.

How do I move my collection to another installation?

Open Settings → Storage & backup → Export collection backup. Keep that ZIP, install the new app, then choose Restore collection backup there. Verify your restored collection before removing the old app. Add restored packs to WhatsApp again from the new installation.

Why does a new WhatsApp pack need three stickers?

A new pack needs three stickers for its first import and holds up to thirty. You can update an existing pack with one more sticker. When a pack is full, choose or create another pack for the stickers you select.

Does deleting an original remove its pack copies?

No. Original captures and prepared pack copies are stored separately. Delete originals from Stickers or a preview; use pack management to remove pack copies. WhatsApp’s cached copy may need removing within WhatsApp too.

What should I include in a support message?

Include the app version, phone model, Android version and exact error wording. Captures, backups and private messages are optional; send them only if you intend to share them with support.
